# Authentic research-need intake form: university-branch extract

## Instructions for the respondent

Describe a real problem you are researching or addressing. You do not need to know which discipline to approach or provide any expert names. You may write “unclear” for items you do not know; do not invent metrics to fill the form. One page of text is usually sufficient.

Use an anonymized version that you are permitted to share; do not include personal contact details, undisclosed technical details or material not authorized for research use. Before actual research collection, the project lead must complete the applicable research-approval and consent procedures.
